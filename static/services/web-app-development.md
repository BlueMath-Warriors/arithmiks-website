## FAQs

**Questions we hear before we start**

**How long does a web app take to build?**
Most MVPs go live in 4 to 5 months: discovery and design first, then two-week sprints, then a short integration and validation phase. You get a firm timeline at the end of discovery.

**Do you add AI to every web app?**
No. We add AI where it removes real work, like search, support answers or report drafting. If a feature works better without AI, we’ll tell you in discovery.

**How much will it cost?**
It depends on scope, users, integrations and AI usage. Discovery ends with a roadmap and estimate in writing, so you know the cost before development starts.

**Will our data be used to train AI models?**
No. We use providers and settings that keep your data out of model training, and AI features answer from your own content.

**Can you work on our existing web app?**
Yes. We review the codebase first, fix what slows it down, then add features or AI in sprints without a full rebuild.

**What happens after launch?**
Ongoing support: bug fixes, security updates, speed and AI cost monitoring, and new features planned from how people use the app.
