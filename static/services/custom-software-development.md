## FAQs

**Questions we hear before we start**

**How long does custom software take to build?**
Most first releases go live in 4 to 6 months. Discovery and design come first, then development in two-week sprints with testing alongside. You get a firm timeline at the end of discovery.

**Should we build custom or buy off-the-shelf?**
If an off-the-shelf tool already covers your process, we’ll tell you. Custom makes sense when your workflow is what sets you apart, or when you’re paying for several tools stitched together. Discovery answers this before you commit.

**How much will it cost?**
It depends on scope, users, integrations and AI usage. Discovery ends with a roadmap and estimate in writing, so you know the cost upfront.

**Can you work with our existing systems?**
Yes. Most projects connect to tools you already run, such as an ERP, CRM, accounting or payment system. We build those integrations with monitoring, so your data stays in sync.

**Who owns the code?**
You do. The code, designs, prompts and infrastructure are yours from day one, and we hand over the documentation and access you need to keep building.

**What happens after launch?**
We offer ongoing support: bug fixes, security updates, AI accuracy and cost monitoring, and new features planned from how your team uses it.
