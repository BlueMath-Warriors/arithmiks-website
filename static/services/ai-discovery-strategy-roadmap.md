## What is AI Discovery, Strategy & Roadmap?

AI Discovery, Strategy & Roadmap helps businesses identify where AI can solve real operational problems, and where it may not be the right fit.

We start by understanding how your business works, the challenges your teams face, and where time or resources are being lost. From there, we identify practical AI opportunities and assess what it would take to implement them.

### What you bring

Business challenges · Inefficient workflows · Data problems · An AI idea

### Discovery Call (1–2 days)

- **AI Possibilities** — Where AI could potentially fit into your business.
- **AI Opportunities** — The areas with the strongest potential for improvement.
- **Next Steps** — A practical direction for what to explore next.

### AI Roadmap (1–2 weeks)

- **Feasibility** — Can AI realistically solve the problem?
- **Data & Integrations** — What data it needs and how it connects to your systems.
- **Governance** — How the solution is managed and used.
- **Solution Design** — What the AI solution looks like in practice.
- **Path to MVP** — Estimated time and cost to implementation.

### Build (5–6 weeks)

- **Working Prototype** — Tested against your real business data.
- **Live AI MVP** — Built around the agreed roadmap.
- **Adoption Plan** — From testing toward everyday use.

## FAQs

**Asked before we start**

**What is AI Discovery Strategy & Roadmap?**
It is a structured process for identifying potential AI opportunities in your business, evaluating their feasibility, and creating a practical roadmap for implementation.

**Do I need to already have an AI use case?**
No. You can come with a business challenge, an operational problem, or an idea you're considering. The discovery process helps identify where AI may be useful.

**How long does the process take?**
The Discovery stage takes 1–2 days, the AI Roadmap takes 1–2 weeks, and the Build stage takes approximately 5–6 weeks.

**Will you assess our existing data?**
Yes. Data requirements and integrations are considered during the AI Roadmap stage, and the selected use case is validated against real data during the Build stage.

**Will we know the estimated cost before development?**
Yes. The AI Roadmap includes a delivery plan with an estimated cost and timeline for implementation.

**What happens after the AI Roadmap?**
If the opportunity is validated and you're ready to move forward, the Build stage can take the selected use case into prototyping and AI MVP development.

**Do we need to use a specific AI provider?**
Not necessarily. The Build offering includes an in-house AI MVP template designed to work with multiple AI providers, giving you greater flexibility and visibility into AI costs.

Anything else, bring it to the first call. It’s free.
