## Why it matters

**Users judge apps in seconds. AI raises the bar.**

- **77%** of daily active users stop using an app within three days of installing it. (Andrew Chen, App retention data — https://andrewchen.com/new-data-shows-why-losing-80-of-your-mobile-users-is-normal-and-that-the-best-apps-do-much-better/)
- **62%** of people uninstall an app after it crashes, freezes or shows errors. (Dimensional Research, User survey — https://techbeacon.com/app-dev-testing/mobile-app-user-survey-failing-meet-user-expectations)
- **88%** of organizations now use AI in at least one business function, so users expect apps that know them. (McKinsey, State of AI, 2025 — https://www.mckinsey.com/featured-insights/charts/ai-at-work-but-not-at-scale)

### Expectations keep rising.

People compare every app to the best one on their phone. If yours is slower, asks them to repeat themselves or crashes once, it gets replaced, often within the first week.

AI has raised the bar again. Users now expect search that understands them, answers in seconds and features that learn their habits, without the app draining their battery.

So we build for speed first, then add AI where it saves taps.

## FAQs

**Questions we hear before we start**

**How long does a mobile app take to build?**
Most apps reach the stores in 4 to 6 months: discovery and design first, then two-week sprints, then device testing and store launch. You get a firm timeline at the end of discovery.

**Native or cross-platform?**
Native suits apps that lean on the camera, sensors or heavy animation. React Native or Flutter suits most others and ships iOS and Android together. We recommend one in discovery.

**Do you add AI to every app?**
No. We add AI where it removes real work, like search, assistants or photo input. If a feature works better without AI, we’ll tell you in discovery.

**How much will it cost?**
It depends on platforms, features, integrations and AI usage. Discovery ends with a roadmap and estimate in writing, so you know the cost before development starts.

**Will our data be used to train AI models?**
No. We use providers and settings that keep your data out of model training, and AI features answer from your own content.

**What happens after launch?**
Ongoing support: crash fixes, OS and security updates, speed and AI cost monitoring, and new features planned from how people use the app.
