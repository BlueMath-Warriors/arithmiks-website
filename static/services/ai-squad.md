## What is an AI Squad?

**Good AI ideas stall. A squad keeps them moving.**

Not a one-off AI project. The squad aligns your commercial goals with technical delivery.

A good idea can still stall: tools applied without priority, experiments on unvalidated assumptions, and no engineering to take a successful test to production. Time goes into projects with no KPIs or measurable ROI.

### Focused and measurable

The squad focuses on one defined AI opportunity. Market-fit criteria, system KPIs and ROI goals are set up front, then reviewed once the system is live.

### A real path to production

As the work grows into a functional business system, senior engineers stay responsible for its architecture, quality and scalability, so a successful experiment becomes a production system that’s ready to scale.
